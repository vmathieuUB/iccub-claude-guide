# How to get Claude

Before you pay for anything, check whether you can join a plan that already exists.

## 1. Ask your group first

**Whether or not you are a PI**, start by asking your colleagues and your PI whether the group already has Claude. Many groups have a **Claude Team plan** with free or spare seats, and your PI can add you in a few minutes. Ask before you buy a plan yourself.

## 2. If you are a PI: the Claude Team plan for scientists

Anthropic offers a **Claude Team plan for scientists** to principal investigators (or equivalent) at universities and non-profit research institutes, in the natural sciences, mathematics, computer science, engineering and related fields.

1. Go to [claude.com/programs/team-plan-for-scientists](https://claude.com/programs/team-plan-for-scientists).
2. Confirm your institutional affiliation (use your UB email) and briefly describe your research.
3. Wait for the review. Most applications are reviewed within **5 to 7 working days**; you get an email when you are approved.
4. Once approved, add the members of your group as seats.

At the time of writing (October 2026), the **Standard** seat is free and the **Premium** seat (5× more usage) is discounted, for the first 12 months, for up to 25 seats per group. Check the program page for the current conditions; promotional pricing is not guaranteed.

## 3. Otherwise: your own Pro plan

If no group plan is available to you, you can subscribe yourself to **Claude Pro** at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) (Settings → Billing). See [What Claude Pro includes](what-is-claude.md) and the [official plan comparison](https://claude.com/pricing).

!!! note "Team plan or Pro: what changes"
    Most of this guide applies to both. A Team plan is a work account managed by your PI (the admin), with shared Projects and central billing, and it is covered by Anthropic's commercial terms rather than the consumer terms described in [Data and privacy](data-and-privacy.md).

Got your account? Continue with [Your first 30 minutes](first-steps.md).
